Donders’ Classic Reaction-Time Experiments

This application demonstrates three tasks: simple reaction time, go/no-go, and choice reaction time. It is intended for teaching and demonstration, not as a validated laboratory measurement tool.

Responses: press Space for the simple and go/no-go tasks; press A or L for the choice task.

Task

Click “Start demo”

Each block begins with brief instructions. Then look at the center of the screen and respond as quickly as possible.

Instructions

1. Simple reaction time: press Space when the stimulus appears.

2. Go/no-go: press Space only for the green stimulus. Do not respond to red.

3. Choice reaction time: press A for red and L for green.

Space = respond
A = red
L = green
